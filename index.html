Privacy Policy — What's Behind?

Last updated: 27 September 2026

What's Behind? ("the game") is developed by VD Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the game.

Data stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your device only: levels completed, stars, coins, lives, photos you have revealed, your chosen nickname and avatar, and your sound and vibration settings. We do not upload this data to our servers, and we do not operate a server that stores player data.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You are never forced to watch an ad; ads are only shown when you choose to. To serve ads, Google may process a device identifier (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address (used to estimate your general location), how you interact with ads, and technical information about your device and app performance. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the game.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases (coin packs and city unlocks), processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play provides us only with a confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Leaderboards and sign-in

If leaderboards are available and you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services, your public gamer profile name and the scores you submit (stars, best score, daily streak) become visible on the leaderboards. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it. Play Games Services is provided by Google and governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Diagnostics

If the game encounters an error, it writes a technical log file to the app's own private storage on your device to help us fix bugs. This file contains no personal data and is not transmitted anywhere automatically.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive basic technical information about your device (such as model, operating system and performance data) to operate and improve the engine. This is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion — What's Behind? (VD Games)

What's Behind? has no user accounts, and VD Games does not store any player data on its own servers. All game progress is stored only on your device.

How to delete your data

  1. Game progress on your device: in the game, open Settings → Reset progress (purchased cities are kept), or go to Android Settings → Apps → What's Behind? → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. Data is deleted immediately and permanently.
  2. Advertising data (Google AdMob): Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. You can also change your ad consent in the game from Settings → Privacy options.
  3. Google Play Games data (only if you signed in to leaderboards): Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Any other request: email vdionisogr@gmail.com with the subject "What's Behind – data deletion". We reply within 30 days.

Data deleted and data kept

Deleted: all game progress and settings stored on your device.
Kept by VD Games: nothing — we hold no player data.
Kept by Google or Unity: data processed by Google (AdMob, Google Play, Play Games) or Unity is retained according to their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Reset all progress from the game's Settings screen.
  • Change or withdraw your advertising consent from Settings → Privacy options (EEA, UK, CH).
  • Uninstalling the game removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game evolves. The date at the top always shows the current version.
